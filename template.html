Month by month

Monthly budget

Moving money isn't spending it

  • Card spending is an estimate: every rise in a credit card's balance from one snapshot to the next. A drop is a payment, which doesn't take spending back, so it's never negative. Accounts with the type "Credit card" count, or with "card" in the name.
  • Money in is the change in your cash accounts (checking, savings) over the month, from the last snapshot before it starts to the last one in it, plus what you paid off on cards: that money was already counted as card spending. Investing and retirement accounts don't count.
  • Left over is money in minus card spending. Averages use complete months only, so the month in progress doesn't drag them down.
  • The budget runs per card from its last payment, the last time its balance dropped. That payment is roughly what the previous cycle cost.
  • It's a beta because it reads balances, not transactions: charges and a payment between the same two snapshots partly cancel, so spending can read low, and a transfer between your own accounts moves money without anyone spending it. More frequent snapshots make it closer.

Help

  • Blank means "not updated". On Update balances, leaving a field blank carries that account's last balance forward; type 0 only when it really is $0.
  • Changes stay in this browser until you press Save to workbook in the bar at the top. Close the workbook in Excel first. Chrome and Edge save straight into it and keep the old file in a Workbook backups folder beside it; other browsers download the updated workbook to put in place of the old one. Then rebuild the dashboard (open it from the launcher, or run build_dashboard.py).
  • Account details and Open/Closed are kept on the workbook's Accounts sheet, so you can change them there too.
  • Owners, their colours, new accounts and the column order are set in the workbook itself: the legend in row 1, each account's header fill, and its columns.
  • Importing replaces everything in this household. After the first import, add snapshots in the app instead, and use Export to Excel now and then as a backup.
  • Account details (type, institution, last 4, notes…) live on Accounts, with their own export and import that never touch balances.
  • Inviting someone here doesn't let them past Cloudflare Access: also add their email to the Household policy in the Cloudflare dashboard.
  • On a shared or borrowed device, use Log out in the menu at the bottom of the sidebar.
  • Undo on Update balances covers snapshot changes made in this browser tab, until it's closed.

Statements

Moving money isn't spending it

When the same amount leaves one of your accounts and arrives in another within a few days, the two are matched as an internal transfer: a card payment, or savings moved across. Neither side counts as income or spending. Set a row's category to undo a match, or pick "Internal transfer" to mark one by hand.

Only what's read is kept

Your browser reads the statement; the file itself is never uploaded or stored. Only each row's date, description and amount are saved, for this household, and long numbers in descriptions (card or account numbers) are cut to their last 4 digits.

Goals live on the hosted dashboard

Goals are saved with your household so everyone sees the same ones, so they need the hosted version. There you can set an emergency fund, a trip or a deposit, a debt paid off by a date, or a net worth target, each measured against these balances.

Category rules

When a description contains the text, the transaction gets that category: for every upload from now on, and for rows already saved that don't have a category yet. The first rule that matches wins.

Colour

Pick any colour, or type a hex code. A colour of your own isn't checked for contrast in light and dark mode, or for standing apart from the other owners, so it may be harder to read. Default always puts the suggested one back.
